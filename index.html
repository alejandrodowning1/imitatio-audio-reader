Add Imitatio to your phone

DE IMITATIONE CHRISTI

iPhone or iPad

  1. Tap Open reader below. If asked, open the page in Safari.
  2. In Safari, tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Open Imitatio from its new Home Screen icon.

Android

  1. Tap Open reader below in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app in the browser prompt, or open Chrome’s menu and tap Install app.
  3. Confirm the installation, then open Imitatio from your Home Screen or app list.
